جامعة نور الهدى

Our motto

To give world-class Islamic education that brings together Deen and Dunya, faith and knowledge of the world, and to carry it to the farthest corners of the earth.

Our final objective is to grow this academy into Jamia Nurul Huda, an online university. It will bridge the gaps that borders, distance and cost put between people and learning, so that good education reaches everyone.

What we stand for

Deen and Dunya together

Quran, Arabic and Islamic studies taught alongside the knowledge children and adults need to thrive in the modern world.

Grounded in knowledge

Teaching built on authentic sources, sound scholarship and scientific knowledge. We encourage questions, evidence and clear thinking.

Against hate and division

We reject anything that spreads hatred or divides people. Our classrooms bring people together, with respect for every human being.

For all humankind

Our doors are open to everyone, whatever their background or religion. Learning is a gift we want to share with the whole of humanity.

Without borders

Online, affordable and available in many time zones, so a child in a small town learns from the same teachers as a child in a capital city.

The road to Jamia Nurul Huda

Each stage builds on the one before it.

  1. Nurul Huda Academy

    Today: one-to-one Quran, tajweed, hifz, Arabic and Islamic studies classes, with tested teachers and protected payments.

  2. Nurul Huda Institute

    Structured courses with certificates, teacher training, and programmes for adults and new Muslims.

  3. Jamia Nurul Huda

    Our final objective: an accredited online university offering diplomas and degrees that join Islamic sciences with modern disciplines, open to learners everywhere.
