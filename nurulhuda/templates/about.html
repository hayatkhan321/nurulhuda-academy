Our Head of Academics

Our Head of Academics is a Hafiza of the Quran. She designs the curriculum for each level, personally reviews every teacher's recitation and trial lesson before approval, and teaches her own students every day.

Her standard is simple: would she trust this teacher with her own child?

Founder and Managing Director

Dr. Hayat Ahmad Khan
Medical doctor. MBA, Manchester Business School.

Dr. Hayat Ahmad Khan is a medical doctor who founded Nurul Huda Academy as a philanthropic service. As Managing Director he leads the academy's development, operations and partnerships, so that our Head of Academics can focus entirely on teaching standards.

He brings two things to the academy: a doctor's habit of care, safety and careful records, and the management training of an MBA from Manchester Business School. Both shape how we protect families' payments, pay teachers on time and keep children safe online.

His aim is the one on our vision page: to grow this academy into Jamia Nurul Huda, an online university that brings world-class education, Deen and Dunya, to anyone who wants to learn.

Why we started

Living in Canada and the UAE, we saw how hard it is for families abroad to find a reliable Quran teacher. Teachers would message families directly, classes started and stopped, and sometimes teachers were never paid for the work they did.

Nurul Huda Academy fixes both sides. Families pay the academy, and we release the teacher's pay only for classes that took place. Teachers are tested before they teach, and they are paid on time, every week.

Our final objective is to grow into Jamia Nurul Huda, an online university that joins Deen and Dunya and is open to everyone.
